# Planning Heuristics Lexicon

## About this document

Rules for project work, operations, and team organization. OPS and TEAM retain
their permanent IDs and original tier and phase cells after relocation.

Tier: **B**locker (violating it predictably makes the stated outcome unattainable
by rewarding contrary behavior or committing scarce capacity to a path that cannot
deliver it), **S**trong default (usually improves focus, coordination, or delivery,
but the lapse remains recoverable), **J**udgment (the choice depends on context).
Phase: **p**lan, **w**rite, **r**eview, **o**ps (ship). The legacy **s**trategy code (plan) is retained for
OPS-58 and OPS-70 so their original phase cells stay intact.

**Example of a row that does not meet B:** [OPS-03](#ops-03) is S: discounting an
advisor's upsell protects a later choice, but the trigger is advice, not an approved
purchase; missing that signal alone neither commits scarce capacity nor makes the
stated outcome unattainable.
