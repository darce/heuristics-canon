# Attribute claims to their bearer

Slug: `attribute-claims-to-their-bearer`
ID: `CARD-18`
Mechanism claim: Every non-visual claim in a description is attributed to the artefact, the source, or the viewer, never to the depicted person as fact-owner.

## Scope

Covers: alt text, captions, audio description scripts, and other access or catalogue prose that names people, bodies, scenes, or stills and that may assert identity, will, affect, guilt, craft motive, or institutional meaning.

Excludes: pure decorative marking with no content claim; pure in-frame inventory that names only checkable visibles; visual layout and contrast mechanics; pipeline provenance outside image language; project tickets or product metrics.

## Observable triggers

- A depicted person is grammatical subject of a trait, will, essence, guilt, faction, or social-identity claim with no attributive frame.
- Caption or alt co-voices non-visible narrative, private affect, or symbolic reading with unmarked inventory of objects.
- Harm, violence, or grief co-occurs with compositional praise or intentional-horror rhetoric stated as in-frame fact.
- Event, purpose, or procedure language leads while high-salience control, body, or force cues are omitted.
- Rank or itinerary words (`emphasizes`, `focus`, `main`, ordered walkthrough) report hierarchy the picture does not privilege by crop, scale, or contrast.
- Craft, staging, candidness, or motive is smuggled into `shows` / `documents` / `captures` without a craft frame.
- A source already names, withholds, or marks illustrative-versus-singular status, and the text rewrites that decision.

## Causal mechanism

Readers treat unmarked descriptive prose as what the frame shows. When the system places non-visual content on the depicted person, that confidence transfers from surface inventory to psychology, identity verdict, and institutional story the still cannot secure. Non-seeing users inherit the false map most of all, because they cannot cross-check the picture. Attribution to artefact, named source, convention, or viewer keeps the claim checkable; omission of bearer converts speculation into evidence.

## Required action

While any trigger holds:

1. Keep FORENSIC voice to in-frame visibles: surface, spatial relations, past presence, and pictorial cues that license rank.
2. For every non-visual claim, name the bearer: viewer reading, convention, artefact construction, catalogue or cited source, or EDITORIAL / INTERPRETIVE frame. Never make the depicted person fact-owner of will, essence, uncited identity, or guilt.
3. Split craft and production claims from depicted biography; label them as making, not as unmediated show-through.
4. Inventory visible relations of force and body before attributed event, purpose, or justification language.
5. Under distress, emit stubborn literal inventory; tag affect, pity, beauty rhetoric, and forced moral plot to viewer, institution, or source.
6. Honor source identity decisions already present; do not invent names, mint plight-types, or swap illustrative and singular status.
7. If no bearer and no checkable warrant exist, cut the clause.

## Predicted failure

The system asserts inner states, relationships, guilt, and significance it cannot know, in the confident register readers and AT users trust most. Swapped side-labels, objectification laundered as attitude, caption story read as pixel evidence, institutional purpose indifference to the photo, and false maps of what the image privileges all follow from unmarked bearer theft.

## Worked example

Catalogue alt for plate 14 listed dress, chair, and window light. A PR draft swaps it for the unmarked claim that the sitter looks resigned after isolation. Keep the inventory in FORENSIC voice; if affect stays, label it as a viewer reading. Screen-reader users otherwise take private psychology as what the frame itself shows.

## Exemptions and boundaries

- Decorative images marked empty-equivalent need no person-claim discipline; once content purpose appears, this card applies.
- A cited catalogue or named record may carry identity or event claims in labelled EDITORIAL voice; the exemption is the citation, not free uncited FORENSIC verdict.
- When production cues are absent, do not invent craft narrative; silence on motive is allowed.
- Sibling concern: how much a still can assert at all (cause, sequence, fate, off-frame total) lives under assertable-set bounds; this card owns *who owns the claim*, not the full inventory of still limits.
- Sibling concern: restaging versus erasure under forced undress or pose is a separate injury-address cut; still require attribution when affect or guilt appears.
- Sibling concern: alt must still serve purpose for non-seeing users; attribution is how purpose stays honest, not a license to under-describe visibles.

## Disconfirmers

- Independent second viewers routinely verify the contested clause from the image alone without shared convention.
- Named source records already state the identity, event, or purpose claim, and the text only carries that attribution.
- Pictorial cues (crop, scale, contrast, known framing) independently license every rank or emphasis word used.
- Removing attributive frames does not change reader or AT interpretation of who owns will, guilt, or meaning.
- For pure decorative or empty-purpose images, bearer discipline never fires because no content claim exists.

## Verification

- Diff every person-as-subject clause: is the predicate a visible cue, or a trait/will/identity/guilt claim? If the latter, is the bearer named?
- Ask: can a second viewer falsify this sentence from the image alone? If no, require source tag or cut.
- Strip beauty words, affect epithets, and intentional parallels; confirm remaining inventory is visible and that judgments name an owner.
- Check order: visible force/body inventory before institutional purpose language.
- Confirm source name/withhold/illustrative status is preserved, not rewritten.
- Confirm emphasis words map to pictorial cues or are labelled editorial selection.
