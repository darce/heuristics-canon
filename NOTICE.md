# Licensing and rights

This notice states what the repository offers under open license and what remains
outside that grant. Nothing here is a legal conclusion about fair use or
downstream redistribution rights.

## Own expression (CC BY 4.0)

Original rule, principle, and reasoning-card prose in this repository is offered
under **CC BY 4.0**:

| Material | Location |
|---|---|
| Rule rows | `lexicons/*.md` |
| Convergence principles | `PRINCIPLES.md` |
| Reasoning-card prose | `reasoning/**` |
| Entrypoint prose | `README.md`, `AGENTS.md`, this notice |
| Bibliography registry | `SOURCES.md` |

A rule states a condition and the action it calls for. What it takes from a
source is the *idea*, not the source's sentences. Material that reproduces a
source's phrasing rather than restating its mechanism is a defect.

`SOURCES.md` lists works and what they feed. It is a registry, not licensed
source text and not chapter-level commentary.

## Outside the CC BY grant

The CC BY 4.0 grant covers **only** the original rule, principle, and card prose
(and the entrypoint templates). It does **not** cover:

- third-party books, papers, standards, or other works identified in
  `SOURCES.md` or rule `Src` cells;
- any quotation, paraphrase, or chapter-level condensation of those works;
- third-party marks, titles, or identities used only for citation.

Cited works remain the property of their authors and publishers. Citation is
not a license to reproduce expression. Obtain original works through ordinary
legal channels.

## Integrity

Published releases may include `meta/release-manifest.json` with SHA-256 digests
for lexicons and reasoning files and the published rule-ID set. Pin a release
tag and verify digests when integrity matters. See [AGENTS.md](AGENTS.md).
